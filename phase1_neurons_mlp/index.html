About Me

My Journey

Physiotherapist | Healthcare Advocate | Marketing Strategist

I am Dr. Alia Misbah, currently serving as Chief Marketing Officer at Paradise Travels & Tours, with over a decade of experience spanning healthcare, marketing, and social impact.

Beyond marketing, I am a dedicated clinician physiotherapist offering specialized services tailored to individual needs. My background in medicine enables me to provide compassionate care through personalized home visits or online consultations.

My professional journey includes diverse roles—from intern at HealthNest to managing sales at Paradise City Nowshera. As a Youth MPA at the Federal Youth Parliament KPK and volunteer at Blood Chain Pakistan, I've been committed to driving positive social change.

I hold a Doctor of Physical Therapy (DPT) degree from Khyber Medical University and specialize in manual therapy, dry needling, spinal manipulation, and advanced rehabilitation techniques.
